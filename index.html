About Federal University Gashua

Federal University Gashua (FUG) is a premier institution of higher learning located in Yobe State, Nigeria. Established with a vision to provide quality education and foster academic excellence, FUG has become a beacon of knowledge in the northeastern region of Nigeria.

Our university is committed to nurturing future leaders, innovators, and problem-solvers through rigorous academic programs, cutting-edge research, and a vibrant campus life. With state-of-the-art facilities and dedicated faculty members, we provide students with an environment that promotes intellectual growth and personal development.
